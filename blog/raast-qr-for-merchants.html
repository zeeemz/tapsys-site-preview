Raast QR for Merchants: Pakistan’s Instant Payment System Explained

What Raast is, how person-to-merchant QR works, what it costs — and how to get a code for your shop.

What Raast is: Raast is Pakistan’s national instant payment system, built by the State Bank of Pakistan. It moves money directly between bank accounts in seconds, around the clock. Its person-to-merchant (P2M) QR standard lets any customer with any Pakistani banking or wallet app scan one QR code at your counter and pay you instantly.

Why Raast QR is different from older QR codes

Before Raast, a shop might need separate QR stickers for each wallet — one for JazzCash, one for Easypaisa, one per bank app. Raast QR is interoperable: one sticker, every app. Payments go straight from the customer’s account to your merchant account — no intermediary wallet balance to sweep, no waiting for a settlement window.

What it costs

Raast P2M is designed for low- or zero-cost merchant acceptance. On top of that, the government’s Rs 3.5 billion subsidy (approved September 2025) reimburses merchants up to 0.5% of each qualifying QR transaction while the scheme lasts — for most small merchants that covers the full cost of acceptance. Card payments, by contrast, carry card-scheme interchange, which is why many shops price QR as the everyday option and keep cards for larger tickets.

How to get a Raast QR for your shop

  1. Choose a provider — your bank, a wallet, or a merchant acquirer like Tapsys (which pairs Raast QR with POS, SoftPOS and a Soundbox under one merchant relationship).
  2. Complete merchant KYC — CNIC, business proof and bank account details.
  3. Receive your QR — a stand or sticker printed with your merchant QR, plus access codes for tracking payments.
  4. Place it where customers pay — counter, table, or parcel for COD businesses.
  5. Optional but recommended: a Soundbox device announces each payment aloud, so staff never have to check a phone screen to confirm a sale.

FAQ

How do I get a Raast QR code for my shop?
Sign up with your bank or a payment provider for Raast person-to-merchant (P2M) QR. After merchant KYC you receive a QR stand or sticker that works with every Pakistani bank and wallet app; funds settle directly to your business account. Providers like Tapsys complete onboarding and can bundle a Soundbox for audio confirmation.
Is Raast free for merchants?
Raast person-to-merchant payments are designed to be low or zero cost, and the government’s Rs 3.5 billion subsidy (approved September 2025) reimburses merchants up to 0.5% of each qualifying QR transaction, making acceptance effectively free for small merchants while the scheme lasts.
Does one Raast QR work with all wallet apps?
Yes. Raast QR is interoperable: a single Raast person-to-merchant QR code can be scanned by customers of any Pakistani bank or wallet app, unlike older wallet-specific QR codes.
