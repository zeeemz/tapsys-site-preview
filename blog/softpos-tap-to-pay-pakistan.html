SoftPOS & Tap-to-Pay in Pakistan: Accept Card Payments on Your Phone

No card machine, no rental, no counter space — how tap-on-phone acceptance works and who it suits.

What SoftPOS is: SoftPOS (also called tap-on-phone) is technology that turns a smartphone into a payment terminal. Instead of a separate card machine, your NFC-enabled Android phone securely accepts contactless debit/credit cards and mobile wallets — the customer simply taps their card on the back of your phone. The Tapsys Merchant Mobile App adds QR codes and Raast payments in the same app.

Why merchants in Pakistan are switching

  • Zero hardware cost. No terminal to buy, rent or maintain — decisive for new and small businesses testing card acceptance.
  • Instant setup. Install the app, complete onboarding, start accepting.
  • One device, every method. Tap cards, wallets, QR and Raast from one screen.
  • Portable. Delivery riders, market stalls, pop-ups and home businesses can accept cards anywhere there's connectivity.
  • Upgrades cleanly. When card volume grows, add a countertop POS terminal without changing providers.

SoftPOS vs POS terminal vs mPOS

SoftPOSMobile POS (mPOS)POS terminal
HardwareYour phone onlyReader + your phoneCountertop machine
Upfront costNoneLowHigher
Best forSmall/new merchants, deliveryMulti-location, eventsSteady counter card volume
Card typesContactless (tap)Contactless + chipFull: chip, tap, swipe

Note: SoftPOS accepts contactless payments; customers with older chip-only cards still dip into a terminal — one reason merchants often run SoftPOS + QR together and add a terminal later.

How to start

  1. Check your phone supports NFC (Settings → search "NFC").
  2. Apply for a merchant account with a SoftPOS provider — Tapsys onboards merchants with CNIC, business proof and bank details.
  3. Install the Tapsys Merchant Mobile App from Google Play.
  4. Start accepting: tap cards, wallets, QR and Raast from one app.

FAQ

Can I accept card payments on my phone in Pakistan?
Yes. SoftPOS (tap-on-phone) apps such as the Tapsys Merchant Mobile App use your Android phone’s NFC to accept contactless debit/credit cards and wallets — no card machine, no rental. The same app also supports QR and Raast payments.
What do I need for SoftPOS?
An NFC-enabled Android phone, a merchant account with a SoftPOS provider (KYC: CNIC, business proof, bank account), and the app. No extra hardware.
Is tap-on-phone secure?
Yes. Reputable SoftPOS implementations are certified against card-industry security standards and use end-to-end encryption; the Tapsys Merchant Mobile App is deployed under Tapsys’s PCI DSS approved environment.
