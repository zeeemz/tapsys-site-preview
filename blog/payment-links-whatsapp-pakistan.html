Payment Links: Collect Payments on WhatsApp in Pakistan — No Website Needed

Send a link, get paid by card or wallet. The simplest digital payment method for WhatsApp sellers, service businesses and COD operations.

Short answer: a payment link is a URL you send to a customer — on WhatsApp, SMS, Messenger, Instagram DM or email. They tap it, pay by debit/credit card or mobile wallet, and you get confirmation. No website, no card machine, no POS. Tapsys merchants send payment requests like this straight from their merchant account.

Who payment links are for

BusinessHow the link is used
WhatsApp / Instagram sellersConfirm the order in chat, send the link, hold stock once paid
COD e-commerceRider sends or shows the link at the doorstep; customer pays by card/wallet instead of cash
Tuition, clinics, salonsSend the invoice amount as a link after the session or at month-end
B2B / agenciesRequest advance or milestone payments without sharing bank details every time
Home businesses & freelancersCollect from customers anywhere in Pakistan without any hardware

Why links beat bank transfers for casual sellers

Bank transfers make the customer do the work — open their app, add your account, type the amount, send you a screenshot, and you verify it before shipping. A payment link flips that: you set the amount, the customer taps once, and confirmation reaches you automatically. Fewer typos, fewer “I sent it, check again” conversations, and an automatic record of every sale.

How to start

  1. Open a merchant account with a provider that offers payment requests (Tapsys includes them alongside QR, POS and SoftPOS).
  2. Send a payment request with the amount and a note (order number, invoice reference).
  3. The customer pays by card or wallet; you receive confirmation and can fulfil immediately.

FAQ

How do I collect payment on WhatsApp in Pakistan?
Use payment links (payment requests). You send a secure payment URL to the customer on WhatsApp; they tap it and pay by card or mobile wallet, and you receive confirmation. Tapsys merchants can send payment requests by SMS, email, Messenger or WhatsApp directly from their merchant account — no website required.
Do I need a website to accept online payments?
No. Payment links let any business collect remotely without a website: the customer taps the link and pays by card or wallet. A website with a checkout (payment gateway) simply automates the same collection for stores with many online orders.
Are payment links useful for cash-on-delivery businesses?
Yes. COD sellers use payment links at the doorstep: instead of carrying cash, the rider sends or shows a payment link, the customer pays by card or wallet instantly, and delivery proceeds. Some sellers also pre-collect by sending the link when the order is confirmed.
