What goes into your pricing

Setup & onboarding

Merchant account setup, KYC and device deployment (POS terminal, mobile POS or Soundbox). SoftPOS needs no hardware at all — just your Android phone.

Transaction fees

A per-transaction charge (MDR) that varies by payment method — QR/Raast, domestic cards, international cards or wallets — and by your monthly volume.

Settlement

Funds from your transactions are settled to your business bank account on an agreed cycle. Raast QR payments settle instantly.

Government incentive

The SBP QR subsidy can offset up to 0.5% per transaction

In September 2025, the government approved a Rs 3.5 billion subsidy to promote merchant QR payments. Merchants can be reimbursed for up to 0.5% of each qualifying QR transaction — effectively making QR acceptance close to free for small merchants while the scheme lasts.

Source: State Bank of Pakistan / Finance Division announcements, September 2025. Scheme terms are set by the government and may change.
