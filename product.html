FAQ

Product questions

What is the difference between POS, Mobile POS and SoftPOS?
A POS terminal is a standalone card machine for your counter. Mobile POS pairs a compact card reader with your phone or tablet. SoftPOS (the Tapsys Merchant Mobile App) needs no extra hardware at all — it turns your NFC-enabled Android phone into a card acceptance device and also supports QR and Raast payments.
Which Tapsys product is right for a small shop?
Most small merchants start with QR payments via the Soundbox or the Merchant Mobile App, then add card acceptance (POS or SoftPOS) as card demand grows. Talk to our team and we will match the mix to your footfall and average ticket size.
Does the Merchant Mobile App support Raast?
Yes. The app integrates Raast — Pakistan’s instant digital payment system — alongside NFC tap-to-pay, QR codes and SoftPOS cardless acceptance.
