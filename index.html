FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Tapsys?
Tapsys (Private) Limited is a Karachi-based payment acceptance company founded in 2020. It gives Pakistani merchants one relationship for accepting electronic payments: POS terminals, mobile POS, a merchant mobile app with NFC tap-to-pay, QR and Raast support, the Soundbox confirmation device, and remote payment links.
How can my business start accepting digital payments with Tapsys?
Contact our sales team through the form, email info@tapsys.net or call UAN 021-111-827-797. We help you choose the right mix of QR, POS, mobile POS or SoftPOS for your business and complete your merchant onboarding.
Which payment methods can I accept?
Domestic and international debit and credit cards, mobile wallets, QR payments (the Merchant Mobile App supports Raast instant payments) and NFC contactless payments via tap-on-phone.
Do I need a website to collect payments?
No. Besides online checkout for websites and apps, you can send payment requests to customers by SMS, email, Messenger or WhatsApp, and accept payments in person with QR codes, POS terminals or tap-to-pay on your phone.
Is Tapsys secure?
Yes. Tapsys is PCI DSS approved, providing a secured payment environment, and its products use end-to-end encryption for transactions.
